# Mountain huts in Bulgaria: what can you actually expect?

A Bulgarian mountain hut is not a hotel — and that is the point. Dorm, bunks, wool blankets, a long table, a hut keeper who remembers your name. Hot water is not a promise. The soup is. This piece describes the dorm as it is.
