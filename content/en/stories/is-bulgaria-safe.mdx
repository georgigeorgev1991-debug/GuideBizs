# Is Bulgaria safe to hike?

Bulgaria is an EU country with marked mountain paths, mountain rescue and huts that are staffed in season. The risk is the high mountains themselves: weather, knees, a misstep on granite. Read how we keep the group small, when we take a local guide, and what you insure yourself.
